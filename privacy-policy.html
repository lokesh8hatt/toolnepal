Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

ToolNepal ("we", "us", "our") operates toolnepal.com. This page explains what information is collected when you use our website and tools, and how it is used. By using ToolNepal, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Client-side tools — your files never leave your device

Nearly all ToolNepal tools (Image Compressor, Watermark Remover, PDF Merge/Split/Compress/Rotate, QR Code Generator, Barcode Generator, all calculators and text converters) run entirely inside your browser using JavaScript. When you select a file or type into a tool, that data is processed on your own device and is never uploaded, transmitted, or stored on our servers. We simply have no access to it.

2. Information we do collect

We collect a limited amount of information to operate and improve the site:

  • Local storage (on your device only): your theme preference (light/dark), a short list of recently used tools, and a small internal page-view/ad-click log used to understand which tools are popular. This data stays in your browser's local storage and is never transmitted to us.
  • Vercel Web Analytics: our hosting provider, Vercel, collects aggregated, privacy-friendly analytics (page views, referrers, approximate location, device type) to help us understand traffic. This does not use cookies and does not track you across other websites.
  • Contact form: if you submit the contact form, your name, email address, and message are sent to our support inbox via a third-party form processor (FormSubmit.co) so we can respond to you. We do not use this information for marketing.

3. Cookies and advertising

ToolNepal may display advertisements served by Google AdSense and other third-party ad networks. These vendors may use cookies (including Google's DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also review how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.

4. Third-party services we rely on

To keep tools fast and free, some pages load external libraries and fonts from third-party CDNs, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Fonts — for the site's typeface.
  • jsDelivr, unpkg, cdnjs — for open-source libraries used by specific tools (e.g. QR/barcode generation, PDF processing).
  • FormSubmit.co — to deliver contact form submissions to our email.
  • Vercel — our hosting provider and analytics.

5. Children's privacy

ToolNepal does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information (for example, via the contact form), please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your choices

You can clear locally stored data at any time by clearing your browser's site data for toolnepal.com, or by using your browser's private/incognito mode. You can also disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect ad personalization.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect immediately upon posting to this page, and the "Last updated" date above will be revised accordingly.
