Our Story

Built for everyday Nepal 🇳🇵

The internet is crowded with slow, ad-ridden, and complicated websites that ask for your email just to resize a photo. We started ToolNepal with a single mission: to build the cleanest, fastest, and most reliable digital tools specifically tailored for students, professionals, and households in Nepal.

⚡ Blazing Fast

We don't rely on bloated servers. Most of our tools run entirely client-side using modern browser technologies. That means calculations, conversions, and image compressions happen instantly, without relying on slow internet speeds.

🔒 Zero Upload Promise

Privacy is our absolute priority. When you compress your citizenship card photo, scan a PDF, or calculate your GPA, your data never leaves your phone. We do not upload, save, or track your sensitive files on any database.

🎯 Zero Complexity

No sign-ups. No pop-up videos. No confusing navigation. We believe software should be invisible—you just open the tool, get your result, and go on with your day.

🇳🇵 Local Context

From TU/BCA CGPA formulas to Bikram Sambat date conversions and Nepal's exact 13% VAT brackets, our logic is hardcoded strictly for the Nepali ecosystem.

Join the Movement

We are constantly adding new features based on what students and creators in Nepal desperately need. If you have an idea for a tool that would save you an hour a week, let us know!
