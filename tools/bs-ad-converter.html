About BS Calendar

Bikram Sambat (BS) is the official calendar of Nepal. It is approximately 56 years and 8 months ahead of the AD calendar.

BS year starts in mid-April (Baisakh 1) of the AD calendar.

Each BS month has 29–32 days, varying by year.

Accurate Bikram Sambat (BS) to English (AD) Date Converter

The Bikram Sambat (BS) calendar is the official calendar of Nepal. It is historically roughly 56 years and 8.5 months ahead of the Gregorian (AD) calendar. However, converting dates manually is incredibly difficult because Nepali months vary between 29 and 32 days depending on the specific year, completely unlike the fixed AD calendar.

How does this Date Converter work?

Our BS to AD Date Converter uses a lookup table with the number of days in every Nepali month from 2000 BS to 2099 BS, cross-checked against two open-source Nepali date libraries and known Baisakh 1 dates. Dates are calculated as whole calendar days, so the result is the same in every time zone. Years far in the future are projections and can shift by a day once the official patro is published, so double-check important dates.

Common Uses in Nepal

  • Convert your Nepali citizenship (Nagarikta) issue date to English for passport or visa applications.
  • Find out your exact English birth date from your Nepali birth certificate.
