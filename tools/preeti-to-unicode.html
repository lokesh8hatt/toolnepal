About Preeti format and Nepali Unicode

Preeti is the most widely recognized legacy typing font in Nepal. For decades, typists, government officials, and students have trained their fingers to type fast in Preeti. However, Preeti has a fatal flaw in the modern internet age: if the person receiving your document does not have the "Preeti.ttf" font installed on their computer, your beautiful Nepali writing will display as unreadable English gibberish (like "d[xf] a[nsf]").

Enter Nepali Unicode

Nepali Unicode maps Devanagari characters to absolute universal standard codes. When you type in Unicode—or convert your Preeti text to Unicode using our tool—you guarantee that your text will be perfectly readable on any Android phone, iPhone, Windows PC, Mac, Facebook post, or modern website without requiring any special fonts.

  • Zero Limits: Convert entire books or 10-page essays instantly without hitting character limits.
  • 100% Client-Side: Your text is converted instantly in your browser and is never uploaded anywhere, making it safe for confidential government or legal documents.

How to type?

If you are used to typing in Preeti, simply place your cursor in the Preeti box and type as normal (assuming you have a Nepali typing software or mapping). As you type, the tool will instantly spell out the real Devanagari Unicode below.
