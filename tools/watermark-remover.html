How It Works

  1. Upload any image (JPG, PNG, WEBP)
  2. Paint over the watermark area with the brush
  3. Click Remove Watermark
  4. Download the cleaned image
💡 Best Results Use a slightly larger brush than the watermark. The tool samples surrounding pixels so works best on textured backgrounds.
🔒 100% Private Your image never leaves your device. All processing happens in your browser using the Canvas API.
🎨 Multiple Passes For stubborn watermarks, run Remove Watermark 2–3 times on the same area for a smoother blend.
