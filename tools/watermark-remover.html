How It Works

  1. Upload any image (JPG, PNG, WEBP)
  2. Paint over the watermark area with the brush
  3. Click Remove Watermark
  4. Download the cleaned image
💡 Best Results Cover the whole watermark with a small margin. The fill blends colours in from the edges of the painted area, so it works best on plain or smoothly shaded areas (sky, walls, gradients). Detailed textures and objects will look smoothed over.
🔒 100% Private Your image never leaves your device. All processing happens in your browser using the Canvas API.
🎨 Fill Settings Sample Radius sets how far the first edge-inward pass looks for colours. Smoothing then blends the fill to hide streaks and the seam where the edges meet (1 = off). For big or busy areas, work in smaller sections and use Undo if a result looks off.
