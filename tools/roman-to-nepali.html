How does Roman to Nepali Translation work?

Nepali Typing Online has dramatically evolved over the last decade. Previously, if you wanted to type a letter in the Devanagari script, you had to memorize rigid traditional keyboard layouts like Preeti or Kantipur. Today, the Roman to Nepali transliteration engine instantly converts phonetic English spelling into flawless Nepali Unicode text.

Hit Space to Convert

The process is brilliantly simple. As you type a romanized English word (for example, typing nepal), the intelligent live API predicts the phonetic translation and instantly swaps your word into नेपाल the millisecond you hit the Space key, Enter key, or any punctuation mark.

Why Unicode is Better

  • Universal Readability: The Nepali Devanagari script generated by this tool is standard Unicode. Unlike Preeti, it is understood by every single modern browser, smartphone, and social media platform like Facebook or Twitter.
  • Incredibly Fast: It is designed for maximum typing speed. Whether you are searching for Lok Sewa notes, writing a poem in Nepali, or chatting with friends, you can easily reach over 60 WPM seamlessly typing in English phonetics!

Useful Shortcuts

Type your word naturally in English. If the conversion is slightly off for complex words, try spelling it more strictly phonetic.

  • namaste → नमस्ते
  • sanchai → सन्चै
  • chha → छ
