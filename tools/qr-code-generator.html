Why use this Free Local QR Code Generator?

Most QR code generators online trick you into signing up or they create "dynamic" codes that expire after 14 days and demand payment to reactivate. Our ToolNepal QR Code Generator creates static, evergreen QR codes directly in your browser. They will never expire.

Use cases in Nepal

  • Digital Menus: Restaurants and cafes in Kathmandu or Pokhara can generate a QR linking to their PDF menu.
  • Business Cards: Print a QR on your visiting cards connecting to your LinkedIn or portfolio.
  • Wi-Fi Access: Soon we will add Wi-Fi QR support so your guests can scan and connect instantly without asking for passwords.

Because this tool runs entirely on the client-side (in your browser using JavaScript), the content of your QR code is never sent to our servers. It's perfectly safe for embedding sensitive URLs or contact information.
