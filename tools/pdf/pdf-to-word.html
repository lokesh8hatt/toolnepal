PDF to Word

Convert PDF files to editable Word documents (.docx) with formatting, images, and styles preserved.

Features

  • Extracts text with formatting
  • Preserves bold, italic styles
  • Auto-detects headings
  • Extracts embedded images
  • Maintains paragraph structure

Limitations

Scanned PDFs (images only) won't have extractable text. Complex tables and multi-column layouts may not convert perfectly.
