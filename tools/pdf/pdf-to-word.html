PDF to Word

Convert PDF files to editable Word documents (.docx). Text is extracted with bold/italic styles and headings, together with embedded images – all in your browser (no upload).

Features

  • Extracts text into paragraphs
  • Detects bold & italic text
  • Auto-detects headings (by font size)
  • Extracts embedded images
  • Keeps page breaks

Limitations

Scanned PDFs (images only) have no extractable text – you'll get the page images instead. The exact layout isn't reproduced: images are placed in approximate reading order (by vertical position), not at their exact spot, and fonts, colours, tables, multi-column layouts and vector drawings are not preserved.
