Private & Fast Image Compression

Have you ever encountered a "File Size Too Large" error while filling out a government form in Nepal, like a Lok Sewa application, Passport form, or driving license portal? Our Image Compressor easily solves that by instantly shrinking image sizes by up to 90% while retaining visible quality.

100% Private (No Uploads)

Unlike popular compressor websites that force you to upload your sensitive photos (like citizenship cards or passport size photos) to their foreign servers, ToolNepal compresses your image locally inside your device using HTML5 Canvas API.

How to reduce file size?

Simply select your photo, tweak the Quality slider (lower number = smaller file), and optionally set a max-width if the image is physically too large (e.g., a 4000px phone camera photo). Hit compress and download your tiny web-ready image.
