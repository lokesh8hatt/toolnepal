Understanding Nepal's 13% Value Added Tax (VAT)

Value Added Tax (VAT) in Nepal is a consumption tax placed on a product whenever value is added at each stage of the supply chain. The standard VAT rate in Nepal is firmly set at 13% by the Inland Revenue Department (IRD).

How to Calculate VAT manually?

To add VAT (Exclusive to Inclusive): Multiply your base amount by 1.13.
Example: Rs. 1000 base price × 1.13 = Rs. 1130 Total Price.

To remove VAT (Inclusive to Exclusive): Divide your total amount by 1.13.
Example: Rs. 1130 total price ÷ 1.13 = Rs. 1000 Base Price. The VAT amount is Rs. 130.
