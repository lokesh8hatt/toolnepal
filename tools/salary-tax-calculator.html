How is Nepal's Income Tax Calculated?

Navigating the Inland Revenue Department (IRD) tax slabs for the Fiscal Year 2081/82 (2024/25) can be incredibly complex. Many employees in Kathmandu and across Nepal are shocked when their HR drops a large Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) cut from their monthly salary. Using our Nepal Income Tax Calculator, you can accurately predict your take-home pay.

The New Tax Slabs (2081/82)

  • Slab 1 (1%): Up to Rs. 5 Lakhs for singles (Rs. 6 Lakhs for married couples). This is completely waived to 0% if you put your money in SSF or an approved CIT!
  • Slab 2 (10%): For the next Rs. 2 Lakhs.
  • Slab 3 (20%): For the next Rs. 3 Lakhs.
  • Slab 4 (30%): For the next Rs. 10 Lakhs (Single) or Rs. 9 Lakhs (Married).
  • Slab 5 (36%): For any income above Rs. 20 Lakhs per year. This includes the massive 20% extra surcharge directly placed on high-income earners.

Dashain Bonus

In Nepal, most full-time employment contracts distribute a basic one-month salary as a designated Dashain Bonus. Because of this, the yearly calculation uses 13 months instead of 12 months for your gross accumulation. Don't forget to select this in the drop-down to avoid paying surprising tax arrears in Ashadh!
