This is an estimate for resident individuals with salary income. Retirement contributions (SSF + CIT/PF) are deductible up to one-third of income or Rs. 5 lakh, whichever is lower. The 1% social security tax is waived only for SSF / contributory pension contributors. Monthly tax is the yearly tax spread evenly over your salary payments; your employer may deduct the bonus-month tax separately. Rates follow the FY 2083/84 budget (Finance Act 2083) as published — confirm with the Inland Revenue Department before filing.

How is Nepal's Income Tax Calculated?

Your employer deducts Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) from your salary every month based on the Inland Revenue Department (IRD) slabs. This Nepal Income Tax Calculator adds up your yearly salary (including the Dashain bonus), subtracts the allowed deductions, applies the slabs and spreads the tax back over your monthly payments.

Tax Slabs for FY 2083/84 (same for single and married)

  • 1% (Social Security Tax): first Rs. 10 lakh. Waived if you contribute to SSF or a contributory pension fund (not for CIT/EPF alone).
  • 10%: Rs. 10–15 lakh.
  • 20%: Rs. 15–25 lakh.
  • 27%: Rs. 25–40 lakh.
  • 29%: above Rs. 40 lakh.

Tax Slabs for FY 2082/83

  • 1%: first Rs. 5 lakh (single) or Rs. 6 lakh (married).
  • 10%: next Rs. 2 lakh; 20%: next Rs. 3 lakh.
  • 30%: up to Rs. 20 lakh; 36%: Rs. 20–50 lakh; 39%: above Rs. 50 lakh.

Deductions included

  • SSF (11% from you + 20% from your employer, both of basic salary), CIT and PF: combined deduction up to one-third of income or Rs. 5 lakh.
  • Life insurance up to Rs. 40,000, health insurance up to Rs. 20,000, private house insurance up to Rs. 10,000 (Rs. 5,000 in FY 2082/83).
  • Remote area deduction from Rs. 10,000 (Category E) to Rs. 50,000 (Category A).
  • A 10% rebate on tax for women whose only income is salary.

Not included: medical tax credit, foreign income, investment income and other special cases. For anything unusual, check with your employer's payroll team or a tax professional.

Dashain Bonus

Most full-time contracts in Nepal pay one extra month's salary as a Dashain bonus, so the yearly calculation uses 13 payments instead of 12. Select the right option to avoid a surprise tax bill in Ashadh.
