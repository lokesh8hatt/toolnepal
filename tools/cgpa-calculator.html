How to Calculate CGPA in Nepal?

Calculating your Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) can be confusing with different universities (TU, PU, KU) having varying credit hours and grading systems. Our free online CGPA Calculator for Nepal simplifies this process beautifully.

The CGPA Formula

The standard formula used by most Nepali universities (including TU BCA, CSIT, and engineering programs) is:
CGPA = Total Grade Points Earned / Total Credit Hours Attempted

To use this tool, simply input the Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) you received for each semester. The tool averages them out equally. If your university uses dynamic credit hours per semester, you can estimate your final percentage directly from the final result.
