About the Nepali calendar (विक्रम संवत्)

Nepal officially uses the Bikram Sambat (BS) calendar, written विक्रम संवत् in Nepali. It runs about 56 years and 8 months ahead of the Gregorian (AD) calendar, so 2083 BS lasts from mid-April 2026 to mid-April 2027. Government offices, schools, banks and documents such as the citizenship certificate (नागरिकता) use BS dates, which is why so many people check आजको मिति (today's date) every day.

BS months follow the sun: a new month begins when the sun moves into the next zodiac sign (संक्रान्ति). Because of this, a month can have 29, 30, 31 or 32 days, and the same month can be a different length in different years. A full year still has 365 or 366 days. The year starts on Baisakh 1 (बैशाख १), the Nepali New Year, which falls on 13 or 14 April. Month lengths and festival dates for each year are decided by the Nepal Panchanga Nirnayak Samiti (नेपाल पञ्चाङ्ग निर्णायक समिति).

Saturday (शनिबार) is the weekly holiday in Nepal, so Saturdays are shown in red. Festival and public holidays change every year and are announced by the government, so they are not listed here.

Frequently asked questions

What is today's Nepali date?

Today's Nepali date (आजको मिति) is shown at the top of this page in Bikram Sambat (BS), in Nepali and English, together with the English (AD) date. It is worked out from the date on your own device, so it changes automatically every day.

How many days are there in a Nepali (BS) month?

A Bikram Sambat month has 29, 30, 31 or 32 days, and the length of the same month changes from year to year. A full BS year has 365 or 366 days.

When does the Nepali (BS) year start?

The Bikram Sambat year starts on Baisakh 1 (बैशाख १), the Nepali New Year, which falls in mid-April, usually on 13 or 14 April.
